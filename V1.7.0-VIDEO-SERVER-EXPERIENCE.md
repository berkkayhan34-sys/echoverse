# EchoVerse v1.7.0 — Video & Server Experience

This is the first larger post-1.6 release and builds on the stable v1.6.9 voice-lobby sync.

## Video 2.0
- Camera device selector in Ses & Video.
- Live camera switching while the camera is already enabled.
- Camera target profile: 720p / 30 FPS.
- Video toolbar with Grid and Focus viewing modes.
- Speaking video gets a stronger active-speaker highlight.
- LIVE badge on remote video surfaces.

## Screen Share 2.0
- Selectable 720p or 1080p sharing profile.
- Selectable 30 FPS or 60 FPS profile.
- Selected quality is persisted locally.
- Screen-share toolbar reports the active profile.
- Existing Windows/macOS screen-source picker and macOS permission flow are retained.
- Screen share continues to use the established WebRTC sender/renegotiation path.

## Settings
- The old Ses panel is now Ses & Video.
- Microphone, output, camera, screen-share quality, lobby sounds and effect volume live in one place.

## Stability retained
- v1.6.9 server-authoritative lobby member sync.
- Reconnect lobby repair.
- Join/leave sounds.
- Ghost peer cleanup.
- v1.6.8+ dynamic package-version release workflow.

## Next phase
Roles/permissions and fully persistent multiple text/voice channels are intentionally not faked in this build.
They should be implemented server-side with persistence rather than as cosmetic client-only buttons.
