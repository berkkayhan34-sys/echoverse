# Evidence: `MOD-001`

```yaml
id: MOD-001
status: complete
date: 2026-08-29
revision: 1.8.9 candidate
```

Validated owner/admin moderation commands now support kick, ban, timeout, and
unban. Owner removal is rejected; active moderation and privacy-safe audit
events are persisted in both migration variants and exposed to authorized
moderators.

Member listing and report intake are authorization-gated and persisted. Audit
events are pruned after 180 days, moderation actions are budgeted per actor,
and report intake is limited to ten reports per guild/member per hour. Existing
invite lifecycle and kick/ban/timeout/unban flows remain covered by the socket
contract and integration tests.
