# EchoVerse v1.7.4 — Voice, Screen Share & Polish

- Added supplied outgoing-call, call-connected, call-ended, deafen, screen-share and @mention sound assets.
- Existing incoming-call, join/leave, message and mic effects remain.
- Improved display capture constraints for high-detail 1080p-class screen sharing with up to 60 FPS where supported.
- Display audio is requested where the browser/OS exposes it; unsupported platforms continue video-only.
- Added WebRTC screen sender tuning helper for higher bitrate and maintain-resolution behavior.
- Added speaking pulse, screen-share/fullscreen, notification/modal and general UI polish.
- Push-to-Talk intentionally not included.
- Desktop version: 1.7.4.
